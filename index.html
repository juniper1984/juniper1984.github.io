Our Mission

Closing the diagnostic gap with open technology

Over 2 billion people worldwide lack access to basic diagnostic services. In rural clinics and field settings, the specialists, connectivity, and equipment required for early detection simply don't exist. Lives are lost not because solutions are impossible, but because they haven't been built for these environments.

Edge Diagnostic Research develops portable, self-contained AI diagnostic devices powered by edge computing — tools that run entirely on-device with no internet required. We publish all models, datasets, and hardware designs under open licenses, ensuring that the communities and institutions who need these tools can build, adapt, and deploy them freely.

Our flagship project is an edge-deployed skin lesion classifier built on low-cost, commercially available hardware. It's the first step in a broader platform of open-source diagnostic tools designed for the field.

Research Focus

Three pillars of our work

Edge AI Diagnostics

Developing and optimizing classification models that run on portable, low-power hardware accelerators — enabling real-time diagnostic inference in disconnected field environments.

Diverse Datasets

Building representative, clinically annotated medical datasets that reflect the full range of human skin tones and conditions — addressing critical gaps in publicly available training data.

Open Platform

Publishing all software, models, and hardware designs under open licenses — enabling researchers, health ministries, and NGOs worldwide to build and adapt our tools without commercial barriers.

Our Approach

From prototype to field validation

01

Build & Benchmark

Develop edge-optimized models on portable hardware and rigorously characterize performance across lesion types, skin tones, and imaging conditions.

02

Clinical Collaboration

Partner with academic medical centers and dermatology departments to validate device classifications against expert diagnosis in prospective studies.

03

Dataset Curation

Build diverse, IRB-approved datasets with proper consent and clinical annotation — producing public goods that benefit the entire research community.

04

Open Release

Publish models, code, data, and hardware designs under open licenses. Support community adoption through documentation and technical guidance.

Commitment to Openness

Open by design, protected by license

Everything we build is freely available for research, education, and humanitarian use. Commercial use requires a separate license — ensuring our work serves people, not extraction.
